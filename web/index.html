One fire calendar from two satellite generations

MODIS has counted fires from orbit since 2001 and VIIRS since 2012. They do not see fire the same way, and NASA projects the end of MODIS science in 2027. FireSeam converts the MODIS years into VIIRS-equivalent weekly counts, region by region, and says how far each conversion can be trusted.
